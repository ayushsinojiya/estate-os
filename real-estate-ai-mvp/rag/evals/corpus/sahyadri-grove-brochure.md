# Sahyadri Grove — Baner, Pune

> Synthetic demonstration brochure for a fictional project. Not a real development.

## Project overview
Sahyadri Grove is a 4-acre residential project in Baner with three towers of 22 storeys.
The project offers 2 BHK and 3 BHK homes with large decks and cross ventilation.
MahaRERA registration number: P52100054321.

## Amenities

### Clubhouse
A two-level clubhouse of 14,000 sq ft with a rooftop infinity pool, a fully equipped gym,
a yoga deck and an indoor games lounge with table tennis and billiards.

### Outdoor
A 1.2 km jogging track around the central lawn, a half-size basketball court, a cricket
practice net and a senior citizens' garden with shaded seating.

### For children
A toddlers' play zone, a skating rink and a supervised daycare room near Tower B.

## Specifications

### Flooring and finishes
Vitrified tiles of 800 x 800 mm in the living and dining areas, wooden-finish laminate in the
master bedroom, and anti-skid tiles in bathrooms and balconies.

### Doors and windows
Three-track powder-coated aluminium sliding windows with mosquito mesh; laminated flush doors
with a digital lock on the main door.

### Kitchen
Granite platform with a stainless steel sink, provision for a water purifier and piped gas.

## Location advantages
Five minutes to Baner high street, 10 minutes to the Mumbai–Bengaluru highway, 15 minutes to
Hinjawadi IT Park. Nearby: Vibgyor High school (1.5 km), Jupiter Hospital (3 km), Balewadi
High Street (2 km) and the upcoming Baner metro station (800 m).

## Sustainability
Rainwater harvesting, solar water heating for every flat, an organic waste converter and
a sewage treatment plant that supplies water for landscaping.
