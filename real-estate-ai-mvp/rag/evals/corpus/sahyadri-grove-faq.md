# Sahyadri Grove — Frequently asked questions

## When is possession?
Possession of Tower A is planned for December 2027 and Towers B and C for June 2028, as
declared on MahaRERA.

## Are pets allowed?
Yes. Pets are welcome; a dedicated pet park is provided next to the jogging track.

## How many car parking spaces come with a flat?
Every 2 BHK includes one covered car parking space; every 3 BHK includes two.

## What is the water supply arrangement?
Pune Municipal Corporation supply is supplemented by two borewells and a 4 lakh litre
underground tank.

## Which banks have approved the project for home loans?
The project is approved by SBI, HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank. Loan approval depends on the buyer's
eligibility.

## Is there power backup?
Full power backup for lifts, common areas and the clubhouse, and one kW per flat for lights
and fans.

## What are the site visit timings?
The sample flat is open every day from 10 AM to 7 PM, including Sundays.

## Can NRIs buy here?
Yes. NRI buyers can complete the purchase through a power of attorney and an NRE or NRO account.

## सोसायटी मेंटेनन्स कब से शुरू होता है?
मेंटेनन्स पज़ेशन के ऑफ़र लेटर की तारीख से शुरू होता है।

## પાલતુ પ્રાણીઓ માટે શું વ્યવસ્થા છે?
પાળતુ પ્રાણીઓને મંજૂરી છે અને જોગિંગ ટ્રેક પાસે અલગ પેટ પાર્ક છે.
